### AutoNote

![AutoNote Image](/images/autonote.png)

AutoNote is an innovative application that harnesses the power of AI tools to automatically generate comprehensive meeting and class notes from audio input. Designed with students in mind, AutoNote aims to enhance productivity and efficiency by eliminating the need for manual note-taking. By utilizing cutting-edge technologies such as TypeScript and OpenAI Whisper, AutoNote allows students to focus on the content of their lectures and discussions, while the application diligently captures and organizes the key points. With AutoNote, students can effortlessly review their notes at a later time, ensuring that no important information is missed and enabling them to maximize their learning potential.

### HoldMaven

![HoldMaven Image](/images/holdmaven.png)

HoldMaven is a groundbreaking application that revolutionizes the concept of a virtual assistant. With HoldMaven, users can personify themselves through an AI-generated phone service that efficiently handles their tasks and responsibilities. One of the standout features of HoldMaven is its ability to call users back if they are placed on hold, ensuring that no valuable time is wasted waiting in queue. By leveraging the power of Twilio, HoldMaven provides a seamless and intuitive user experience, allowing individuals to streamline their daily activities and focus on what truly matters.

### Piggie

![Piggie Image](/images/piggie.png)

Piggie is a unique budgeting app specifically tailored for a younger audience. Recognizing the importance of financial literacy from an early age, Piggie aims to make the process of budgeting more interactive, engaging, and fun. By utilizing TypeScript and React, Piggie offers a visually appealing and user-friendly interface that encourages young users to take control of their finances. Through gamification elements and intuitive features, Piggie empowers children and teenagers to develop healthy financial habits, setting them up for a successful financial future.

### King Energy

![King Energy Image](/images/king-energy.png)

During my internship at King Energy, I had the opportunity to contribute to the development of a sophisticated file management and tagging system. The primary objective of this project was to streamline the process of file selection and tagging, ultimately reducing the time and effort required for manual file management. By leveraging TypeScript and the Google API, we created a robust solution that significantly enhanced productivity and efficiency within the organization. The system's intelligent tagging capabilities and intuitive interface allowed users to quickly locate and categorize files, eliminating the need for tedious and time-consuming manual tasks.
