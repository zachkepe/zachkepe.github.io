Google FZF Search Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 27, 2025

This privacy policy explains how the Google FZF Search Chrome extension, developed by Zachary Kepe, handles your data. Our commitment is to protect your privacy by ensuring all processing happens locally on your device.

Data Processing

All data processing for Google FZF Search occurs locally within your browser. This includes search queries, web page content, and PDF documents you interact with. The extension uses TensorFlow.js and other local libraries to perform semantic, exact, and fuzzy searches without sending any information to external servers.

Data Collection

No user data is collected, stored, or transmitted to external servers. The extension does not send your search queries, browsing history, or any other personal information to me (Zachary Kepe) or any third party.

Local Storage

The extension uses your browser's local storage solely to remember your preferences, such as your preferred search mode (semantic, exact, or fuzzy). This data remains on your device and is not accessible to anyone else.

Third-Party Services

Google FZF Search does not rely on third-party services for its core functionality. Any external resources (e.g., fonts from Google Fonts) are purely for cosmetic purposes and do not involve data sharing.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the extension, please reach out via GitHub Issues or connect with me on LinkedIn.
